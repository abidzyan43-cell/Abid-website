  • Increase exercise gradually.
  • Get adequate sleep and recovery.
  • Eat enough protein and vegetables.
  • Track your weight and waist.

  • Keep rest days.

  • Stay hydrated.
  • Listen to your body.

🩺 Common Diseases

Click any disease to see basic health and diet awareness information.

Hypertension

Reduce excess salt, eat vegetables and whole foods, maintain healthy activity and follow prescribed treatment.

Diabetes

Control portions, choose high-fibre foods, limit added sugar and remain physically active. Follow prescribed medicines.

Obesity

Focus on sustainable calorie control, protein-rich foods, vegetables, physical activity and adequate sleep.

Anemia

Iron-rich foods may help, but confirmed anemia requires medical evaluation to identify the cause.

Asthma

Avoid known triggers and use prescribed inhalers correctly. Severe breathing difficulty requires urgent medical care.

Dengue

Maintain hydration and medical monitoring. Do not self-medicate with aspirin or NSAIDs unless specifically advised by a clinician.

Malaria

Malaria requires appropriate antimalarial treatment based on diagnosis and medical guidance.

Typhoid

Medical evaluation and appropriate antibiotic treatment may be required. Maintain hydration and food hygiene.

High Cholesterol

Increase fibre-rich foods, reduce unhealthy saturated/trans fats and maintain regular exercise.

Fatty Liver

Healthy weight management, regular physical activity and limiting alcohol can help. Follow medical advice.

Thyroid Disorders

Diet depends on the specific thyroid condition. Take prescribed medicines and attend follow-up tests.

GERD / Acidity

Smaller meals, avoiding personal trigger foods and not lying down immediately after meals may help.

Constipation

Increase fibre gradually, drink adequate fluids and stay physically active.

Diarrhea

Focus on fluids and oral rehydration when appropriate. Seek medical care for severe dehydration or persistent symptoms.

Migraine

Regular sleep, hydration and identifying personal triggers may help. Recurrent severe headaches need medical assessment.

Kidney Disease

Diet must be individualised according to kidney function, sodium, potassium and protein requirements.

Heart Disease

Follow prescribed medicines, avoid smoking and maintain clinician-approved physical activity.

COPD

Avoid smoking and pollutants, use prescribed inhalers correctly and seek care for worsening breathlessness.

Arthritis

Low-impact exercise, strength training and healthy weight can support joint function.

Peptic Ulcer

Persistent pain or bleeding needs medical evaluation. Avoid unnecessary NSAID use.

This information is for general health education and is not a substitute for professional medical care.
