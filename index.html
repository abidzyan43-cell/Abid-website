📖 The Power of Consistency

“Success is not built in one day. It is built by doing the right things every day.”

Consistency does not mean being perfect every day. Some days you will feel tired, unmotivated, or frustrated. Sometimes your progress will seem invisible. The important thing is to keep moving forward.

Small actions, repeated every day, create big changes over time.

• Be 1% better every day.
• Stay disciplined when motivation disappears.
• Learn from failure and start again.
• Trust the process when progress feels slow.
• Never let one bad day become a reason to quit.

Motivation may help you start, but consistency is what takes you to your destination.

“Don’t aim to be perfect. Aim to be consistent.”
